### 2. Cross-session AI recall
**Requested outcome:** Make the charter available across sessions and agents.  
**Implemented here:** A portable, repository-hosted source of truth that a future session or agent can explicitly retrieve.  
**Not established:** Automatic recall in every ChatGPT session, voice session, model, device, or third-party agent. That requires supported memory/configuration mechanisms or a client integration that loads this file, with access controls and tests. Do not represent repository availability as omnipresent recall.

### 3. Global broadcast of Infinite Architect status

**Not established:** A global broadcast, universal recognition, network-wide identity propagation, or live Phoenix federation. ### 4. Dataset and agent charter integration
**Requested outcome:** Make the charter available to datasets and participating agents.

**Not established:** Injection into all datasets, model weights, training corpora, or external systems. Each target needs authorization, a versioned ingestion pipeline, provenance metadata, validation, rollback/fork handling, and a recorded receipt. ## Status vocabulary
- **RECORDED:** written to a versioned artifact.
- **RETRIEVAL-VERIFIED:** fetched again and matched to the intended content.
- **TESTED:** automated test result observed.
- **RUNNING:** live process health and logs observed.
- **FEDERATED:** multiple authenticated nodes exchanged and verified messages.
- **INDEPENDENTLY AUDITED:** an independent reviewer reproduced the evidence.

These labels are not interchangeable. Until evidence is captured, each item remains a target rather than a completed deployment.
