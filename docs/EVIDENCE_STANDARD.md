# Evidence Standard

ZYGROS-PRIME contains research and historical experimental material. Historical experiment plans are not results, and architecture descriptions are not independent validation.

## Evidence levels
- Implemented
- Tested
- Benchmarked
- Verified
- Designed
- Historical

## Experimental discipline
For controlled experiments, preserve the exact commit, dataset, environment, parameters, random seeds where applicable, start/end dates, raw outputs, analysis code, and deviations from protocol. Do not promote an experimental result to production or scientific validation without reproducible artifacts and appropriate independent review.

Trading experiments are research only and must not be represented as financial advice or evidence of future returns.
